Research Interests

01

Connected Speech & Language

Investigating how changes in natural speech and language may reflect subtle cognitive change.

02

Cognitive Aging

Understanding changes in cognition and communication across healthy and pathological aging.

03

Sex-Based Differences in Neurodegenerative Disease

Examining how sex-based differences relate to biomarkers, cognition, and communication in neurodegenerative disease.

04

Cognitive Resilience

Understanding why individuals differ in their ability to maintain cognitive and communication abilities in the presence of aging and disease.
